<Callout type="info" title="How to read a cheat sheet">
**Rank** is the rarity badge top-left. **Class / Role** sits under the name — the class drives team buffs, the role is the job in your five-slot squad. The summary is the one-line verdict, **key skills** are the three abilities to recognise in a report, and the labels underneath are the planning facts: unlock route, best use, team value or upgrade focus. Where a label is missing, the source roster does not state one.
</Callout>

## Classes and roles at a glance

Three classes, three roles, nine combinations — and every hero is built to sit in a specific slot.

| Combination | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Warrior / Tank** | Front line that holds while the squad sets up. Shields, damage reduction, survival. |
| **Warrior / Carry** | Melee damage that converts a stable front line into kills. |
| **Warrior / Support** | Team-wide amplification: attack, damage, or survivability for everyone else. |
| **Warlock / Tank** | Control-resistant front line. Reflection and damage reduction against burst. |
| **Warlock / Carry** | Burn, low-HP pressure and Energy damage that punishes stacked enemies. |
| **Warlock / Support** | Healing and fire damage; the engine behind a Warlock squad. |
| **Ranger / Tank** | Front line with debuffs — lowering DEF or mitigating Energy damage. |
| **Ranger / Carry** | Burst and crit pressure from range, often multi-target. |
| **Ranger / Support** | Buffs on your best attacker plus emergency healing. |

## Building a squad from these cards

1. **Pick one Carry you can actually star up.** The card tells you the unlock route; the route decides how realistic the stars are.
2. **Add the Tank that answers your losses.** Dying to burst? Take damage reduction. Dying to control? Take stun immunity.
3. **Add one Support that feeds your Carry.** Warlock Carry wants a Warlock Support; Ranger Carry wants ATK buffs.
4. **Only then fill the remaining slots.** A fifth hero that duplicates a role is a bench slot with shards sunk into it.

<Callout type="warning" title="Roles are not a tier list">
This page sorts heroes by what they do, not by how good they are. Balance changes and server age move heroes up and down; a clean role description stays useful through both. Pair each card with the [Hero Development](/guides/hero-development/) guide before you commit materials.
</Callout>
