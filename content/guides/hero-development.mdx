<Callout type="strategy" title="The breakpoint rule">
Do not ask "how high can I take this hero?" Ask "what is the next breakpoint, and what does it unlock?" A hero stuck one star below a skill unlock is often less useful than a lower-power hero with a complete kit.
</Callout>

## Roster roles

A functional squad generally needs coverage for:

- **Front line / sustain** — survives the opening rounds.
- **Primary damage** — converts the front line's time into kills.
- **Secondary damage or utility** — fills the gap the main carry cannot.
- **Support / control** — keeps the squad alive or disrupts the enemy.

Exact roles depend on the hero pool and current balance. The principle does not: **coverage beats collection**.

## Investment ladder

| Stage | Goal | Stop when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unlock | Get the hero | Do not spend universal shards on a bench hero |
| Functional | Complete the core kit | Next upgrade is only raw stats |
| Breakpoint | Unlock star/skill tier | Cost exceeds event reward value |
| Specialist | Optimise for a mode | You have no defined use case |

## Universal shards

Universal shards are the most flexible resource in the game and the easiest to waste. Keep them for:

- A hero that completes a role gap.
- A breakpoint that unlocks a skill you need.
- A limited-time hero that will remain relevant.

Do not use them to finish a hero you will replace next week.

## Testing an upgrade

Before and after screenshots are not bureaucracy. They are how you learn:

1. Capture the battle report before the upgrade.
2. Upgrade one variable only.
3. Run a comparable battle.
4. Compare damage, survival and outcome.
5. Keep the result or revert the plan.

## Event overlap

Hero investment is a scoring source in Alliance Duel's Hero phase and in hero-focused events. The best time to spend is when both score the same action — but only if the upgrade is already in your roster plan. An event is a multiplier, not a reason.
