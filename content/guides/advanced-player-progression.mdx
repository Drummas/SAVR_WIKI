<Callout type="strategy" title="Efficiency is the endgame">
Once your account can produce resources reliably, the limiting factor becomes **conversion**. Points per speed-up, damage per resource, reward per event hour. Measure those, and you will outperform bigger accounts.
</Callout>

## From power to conversion

Track these five metrics:

1. **Points per speed-up** by event phase.
2. **Battle delta per resource** for your main composition.
3. **Production efficiency** (actual vs. potential output).
4. **Event reward value** in speed-up equivalents.
5. **Closer reserve utilisation** (spent / held).

A metric that is not written down is a feeling, not a plan.

## Roster depth

Depth is not "more heroes". It is the ability to field a second credible formation without dismantling your first. Build it for a defined purpose:

- A defensive formation for raid weeks.
- A second damage profile for a different opponent.
- A smaller squad for secondary objectives.

If you cannot sustain the second squad's healing and replacement costs, you cannot field it repeatedly. Depth without sustain is a one-time burst.

## Alliance-level impact

Advanced play is team infrastructure:

- Run the ledger so leadership can see real conversion rates.
- Own a phase and train a backup.
- Write the after-action note, not just the battle report.
- Turn personal experiments into shared doctrine.

## Monthly reset

Once a month, remove one habit:

- A spend that consistently underperforms.
- A redundant stockpile.
- A meeting that produces no decision.
- A dashboard nobody reads.

Adding is easy. Subtracting is what creates capacity for the next level.
