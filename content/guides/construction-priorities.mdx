<Callout type="info" title="Build for the account you have">
A construction priority list copied from a max-level player can stall your economy. Priorities are relative to your current bottlenecks, roster and event plan.
</Callout>

## The two-step queue

At any moment, your construction queue should contain:

1. **The next real unlock** — a prerequisite or upgrade that changes what you can do.
2. **A filler** — a cheap, short upgrade to keep the queue busy if the main build completes while you are offline.

This prevents both idle builders and accidental long completions.

## Priority ladder

| Priority | Building type | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Economy production | Funds everything else |
| 2 | Storage | Prevents overflow waste |
| 3 | Unlock prerequisites | Opens new systems and capacity |
| 4 | Training / research capacity | Improves event throughput |
| 5 | Combat / defence | Matters, but only with support |
| 6 | Cosmetic / low-use | Last |

## AvA construction phase

Construction completions are a community-reported scoring source in the Alliance Duel Construction phase. That creates three rules:

- Do not complete discretionary upgrades on the day before the phase.
- Stage long timers to finish inside the window.
- Keep a small instant-finish reserve for the final hour.

## When to break the plan

Priorities change when:

- A new unlock requires a different prerequisite.
- A balance update changes a building's value.
- Your alliance changes its event target.
- A storage or production bottleneck is actively blocking progress.

Breaking the plan for a red dot is not a priority change. Breaking it for a measured bottleneck is.
