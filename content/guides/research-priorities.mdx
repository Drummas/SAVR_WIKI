<Callout type="warning" title="Trees change">
Research trees, prerequisites and node effects change with updates. Use this guide for sequencing logic, then verify the current tree in-game.
</Callout>

## The research queue test

At any login, ask:

- Is the lab researching something useful?
- Will it finish before or after my next login?
- Does that completion time matter for an event?
- Is there a cheap filler node if not?

If the answer to the first question is no, fix that before anything else.

## Branch sequencing

### Economy first when...

- Production is capping or storage is overflowing.
- You cannot sustain the next construction tier.
- Gathering throughput is your bottleneck.
- A capacity node unlocks a useful new action.

### Combat first when...

- Your main squad has a defined troop tier and hero carry.
- A specific node unlocks a meaningful battle stat.
- Raid phase or a state event is the next major objective.
- You can afford the healing and replacement cost.

<Callout type="strategy" title="Research is infrastructure">
Combat research is a multiplier. If your economy cannot replace losses, a combat node is a temporary high. Build the infrastructure that lets you use the multiplier repeatedly.
</Callout>

## Research and event scoring

Community guides report research completions and research Might as Alliance Duel Tech-phase scoring sources. The practical rules:

1. Do not complete a discretionary research node before Tech phase.
2. Stage the final node so it can finish inside the window.
3. Keep research speed-ups for the phase rather than using them as queue filler.
4. Screenshot the point gain to build your own conversion table.

## A two-week planning horizon

Research takes time. Plan at least two weeks ahead:

- Week 1: prerequisites and economy nodes.
- Week 2: target node and combat support.
- Event weeks: align completions, not starting points, with Tech phase.

Finish the plan before starting it. Half-finished research is just a slower version of no research.
