## What "progress" actually means

Power is a symptom, not a strategy. The three engines that carry an account are:

1. **Queue uptime** — builders, researchers and trainers always working.
2. **Resource conversion** — turning production into the next useful upgrade.
3. **Event timing** — spending saved items when they score.

A player who logs in twice a day with full queues will outperform a player who logs in constantly but leaves everything idle.

## Hero direction without overthinking

You do not need a perfect roster in week one. You need a functioning squad:

- One hero who can absorb damage.
- One hero who can deal meaningful damage.
- One support or utility slot if available.
- A plan to finish the next breakpoint before starting a new hero.

Community sources describe battles with up to five heroes. Treat the squad as a system: a second damage hero with no front line is not an upgrade.

## Speed-ups: save first, spend later

New players receive a pile of speed-ups and immediately use them to rush the visible upgrade. Resist. Speed-ups are event currency. Their value is highest when a scored phase rewards the action they accelerate.

Rule of thumb for week one:

- Use only the speed-ups needed to prevent an overnight queue gap.
- Keep a small reserve for a live event you actually understand.
- Never spend premium currency to finish a timer that will complete while you sleep.
