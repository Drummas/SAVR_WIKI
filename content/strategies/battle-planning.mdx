<Callout type="strategy" title="Purpose before target">
"Who can we hit?" is the wrong first question. "What does this hit score, and what will it cost?" is the right one. Target selection is resource allocation with guns.
</Callout>

## Pre-fight brief

Every rally should have a five-line brief:

1. **Objective:** what this attack achieves.
2. **Target:** coordinates and expected defence.
3. **Launch time:** when the rally leaves.
4. **Join cutoff:** latest travel time for fillers.
5. **Second wave:** who leads it and with what reserve.

If the brief takes longer than the fight, the fight is not planned.

<Callout type="info" title="The join window">
Most rally failures are timing failures. Members join late, the rally launches underfilled, or the lead waits so long that the target reinforces. Publish the join cutoff in the same time zone as your members.
</Callout>

## Target priority

| Target type | Value | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Event objective | High | Defended heavily |
| Undefended resource node | Low–medium | Low, but may not score |
| Enemy rally lead | High | Counter-rally |
| Support / reinforcement source | Medium–high | Requires coordination |
| Revenge target | Low | Emotion-driven, often inefficient |

## Rally discipline

- One lead per rally.
- One target per rally.
- One channel for calls.
- A hard join cutoff.
- A second wave planned before the first lands.
- A screenshot of the report.

Duplicate rallies are the fastest way to waste an alliance's stamina.

## Battle report review

Read reports in pairs when possible:

- Before and after an upgrade.
- Your composition vs. a comparable opponent.
- First wave vs. second wave.

Look for:

- Front-line survival time.
- Damage concentration.
- Overkill and wasted attacks.
- Healing cost relative to objective value.
- Whether the win was repeatable.

## When to disengage

Stop the engagement when:

- The objective is already secured.
- The healing cost exceeds the reward.
- The enemy has reinforced beyond your second wave.
- Your rally lead is offline.
- The event window is closing.

A disciplined retreat preserves the army for the next phase. A revenge rally preserves nothing.
