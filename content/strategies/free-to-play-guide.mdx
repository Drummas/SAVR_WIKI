<Callout type="strategy" title="Play the guaranteed game">
Whales compete for ranking rewards. Free-to-play accounts should compete for **guaranteed milestones** and **queue uptime**. Those rewards do not depend on someone else spending more than you.
</Callout>

## The three F2P engines

1. **Queue uptime** — construction, research and training always running.
2. **Guaranteed milestones** — daily and event tiers that everyone can reach.
3. **Overlap efficiency** — one action scoring on two boards.

Rank chasing is a fourth, optional engine. It is expensive and inconsistent. Treat it as a bonus, not a plan.

## Premium currency rules

- Set a floor (for example, enough for one emergency upgrade).
- Spend only above the floor.
- Buy value that compounds: capacity, queue slots, guaranteed progression.
- Never bridge a score gap unless the reward exceeds the currency spent.
- Wait 24 hours before any unplanned purchase.

<Callout type="warning" title="Sunk-cost traps">
Limited-time shops are designed to make the last 20% of a reward feel close. If reaching it requires breaking your premium floor, the correct move is to stop. The shop will return; your reserve may not.
</Callout>

## A weekly F2P routine

| Day | Action | Time |

| Midweek | Check overlap windows, spend only planned actions | 10 min |

## What to ignore

- Leaderboard drama you cannot influence.
- Banners with no roster slot.
- Discounts on resources you already overflow.
- Events whose rewards do not help your next progression step.
- Advice that assumes unlimited speed-ups.

## Measuring success

Track three numbers monthly:

- Guaranteed milestones claimed.
- Premium currency balance trend.
- Main squad and queue progress.

If all three are rising, you are winning the F2P game, regardless of where you sit on a whale leaderboard.
