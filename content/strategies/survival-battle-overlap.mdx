This guide assumes the community-reported structures: six four-hour Survival Battle rounds per day and a Monday–Saturday Alliance Duel phase order. Both are version- and server-sensitive. Open both live event screens before using any overlap map.
</Callout>

## The core idea

Survival Battle is a **short-cycle** scoreboard. Alliance Duel is a **long-cycle** scoreboard.

- Survival Battle round: four hours. The action must land inside that round.
- Alliance Duel phase: one day. The action must land inside that phase.

When the two windows intersect and reward the same action, you get an **overlap window**. That is the only time a single spend has two chances to earn value.

<Callout type="strategy" title="Spend once, score twice">
The goal is not to play more events. It is to make the events you already play share the same action. One training batch, one research completion, one construction finish — placed in the intersection.
</Callout>

## Build the weekly map

Do this every Monday:

1. Screenshot the Survival Battle theme order for the day.
2. Screenshot the Alliance Duel phase and current score.
3. Draw a 7-day grid.
4. Put AvA phases across the top.
5. Put Survival Battle themes down the side.
6. Mark intersections where the same action scores on both boards.
7. Assign stockpiles to intersections, not to individual events.
8. Write the closer reserve and the stop conditions.

## The four-hour round rule

A Survival Battle round is short. You cannot move a 20-hour construction timer into a four-hour window on a whim. You must stage it:

- **T-24h:** start the long timer.
- **T-4h:** check the remaining time.
- **T-1h:** use a controlled speed-up to land inside the overlapping round.
- **T-0:** complete and claim on both boards.

This is why overlap strategy is mostly preparation. The spend happens in seconds; the setup takes a day.

## Overlap decision tree

1. **Do both boards reward this action?**
   - No → Do not spend unless the single-board reward is exceptional.
   - Yes → Continue.
2. **Is the action already planned for the account?**
   - No → Stop. Overlap does not justify a bad upgrade.
   - Yes → Continue.
3. **Is the cheaper threshold action available?**
   - Yes → Use it first.
   - No → Continue.
4. **Will the spend breach the AvA reserve floor?**
   - Yes → Stop.
   - No → Spend, log, claim.

## A worked example

Suppose Tuesday is both Construction phase and a Build Territory round.

- You have a 14-hour build queued since Monday.
- It would naturally finish at 06:00 Tuesday, before the overlap round.
- You use a small speed-up to finish it at 09:30 instead, inside the round and inside the phase.
- You claim both scores and log the resource cost.

The win is not the speed-up. The win is that you **staged the timer** so a small speed-up could move the completion into the intersection.

## What to do when the boards conflict

Sometimes the best Survival Battle action is not scored by AvA, or vice versa. Choose based on:

- **Alliance target:** AvA rank usually outweighs personal bracket rank.
- **Reward value:** compare the actual reward previews, not the headline points.
- **Reserve impact:** protect the resource you need for tomorrow's phase.
- **Morale:** don't ask members to sacrifice a personal milestone for a marginal alliance gain without explaining why.

<Callout type="danger" title="Never spend the closer reserve early">
The reserve exists so the alliance can respond to the final scoreboard. Spending it at midday because an overlap looks exciting removes your ability to close.
</Callout>

## After the week

Record:

- Which overlap windows existed.
- Which were real (both boards scored).
- Points gained per resource.
- What you would stage earlier next week.

One good overlap note is worth more than ten screenshots of final scores. It tells the next plan where to aim.
