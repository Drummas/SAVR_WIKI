## A framework that survives reruns

Limited-time plague events look different each season, but their economy is usually familiar:

1. **A task ladder** generates event currency.
2. **A shop or draw** converts currency into rewards.
3. **A closing time** invalidates anything unspent.
4. **Overlap** with recurring events determines the true cost.

Plan the shop first, then work backwards to the tasks. Most players do the opposite and end up with a currency balance they cannot convert into the reward they wanted.

## Shop discipline

Before buying anything, write down:

- The reward you want.
- Its total cost.
- The currency you can realistically earn from cheap tasks.
- The opportunity cost in AvA stockpiles.

If the reward is not reachable without spending premium currency or gutting your AvA reserve, walk away. Limited-time events rerun; alliance rank does not wait.
