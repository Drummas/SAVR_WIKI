## How the cycle works

Survival Battle is a personal, bracket-based scoreboard. Community documentation reports a daily cycle of **six four-hour rounds**, each with its own theme and related scoring tasks. Your job is not to play constantly; it is to make each themed action land inside its scoring window.

The daily loop has three layers:

1. **Theme score** — actions that match the active theme.
2. **Stage rewards** — thresholds inside each theme.
3. **Daily milestone chests** — fed by Survival Medals across the day.

Treat the three layers separately. Clearing a theme stage does not automatically mean you should stop; missing the daily chest tier by a few medals is a common and avoidable loss.

## Reported theme rotation

| Theme | Typical scored actions | Best preparation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Build Territory | Construction completion, building Might | Pre-stage a long build; hold one small instant finish |
| Train Soldiers | Troop training, tier upgrades | Keep a batch unqueued and speed-ups in reserve |
| Technology Research | Research completion, research Might | Pre-load research before reset |
| Enhance Raven | Raven upgrades and related materials | Save Raven chests/materials for this round |
| Enhance Heroes | Hero upgrades, shards, skill investment | Plan a breakpoint, not a random level |
| Alliance / combat window | Reportedly varies by rotation | Confirm on the live board; do not assume |

<Callout type="info" title="The rotation is not a promise">
Community guides publish a seven-day theme order, but it is not official. Confirm the order in-game each morning. Your plan should be built around **prepared queues**, not memorised days.
</Callout>

## Scoring discipline

The most valuable Survival Battle skill is knowing when **not** to score. Every action you spend here is unavailable for Alliance Duel, a state push or a limited-time event.

Use this hierarchy before any scored action:

- **Free first:** natural regeneration, existing queues, alliance help, claimable dailies.
- **Cheap second:** the smallest speed-up or batch that clears the next threshold.
- **Reserved last:** stockpiles earmarked for AvA phases or limited-time shops.

If a round theme does not match your available actions, accept the lower score. Forcing a bad spend to protect a bracket position is usually a net loss once the next event arrives.

## Reading the bracket

Community guides describe small brackets of similarly progressed players. That changes optimal play:

- You do not need to max every theme to finish well.
- A consistent daily floor often beats one explosive day followed by five empty rounds.
- Bracket rank rewards may be less valuable than the daily milestone chests. Compare the live reward preview before chasing rank.

## Overlap with Alliance Duel

Alliance Duel runs its own scoring phases across Monday–Saturday. When a Survival Battle theme and an AvA phase both reward the same action, that is an **overlap window** — the highest-value time to spend.

Example: if a troop-training theme lands on the AvA Preparation day, one training batch can feed both scoreboards. Confirm both live event screens first, then commit. See [Event Optimisation](/strategies/event-optimisation/) and the [Alliance vs Alliance command centre](/alliance-vs-alliance/).

## When to reset your plan

Rebuild the plan when any of these change:

- A game update changes the theme order or scoring.
- Your server reset time shifts (daylight saving, server merge).
- Leadership changes the alliance's AvA spend targets.
- You unlock a new queue slot or hero breakpoint that changes your action supply.

Keep the old screenshots. They are the baseline for the next cycle.
