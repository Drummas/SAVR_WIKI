## The reserve-first rule

Resource events are the easiest place to damage your alliance's AvA performance. The fix is a written reserve floor:

> **Event budget = current stockpile − AvA reserve floor**

If the event budget is small, score small. A tier-2 reward is not worth entering the next duel with an empty bank.

## What to optimise

| Lever | Why it matters | Cheap action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Production uptime | Free resources while you sleep | Fill queues before logout |
| Gather dispatch | Scores inside the window | Send longest trips first |
| Storage headroom | Prevents overflow waste | Upgrade storage before a push |
| Spending timing | Converts stockpile into score | Spend during the scored phase |
| Reward reinvestment | Compounds future score | Put speed-ups into the next queue |

## A simple 48-hour prep plan

- **T-48h:** stop non-scoring upgrades, fill production, check storage.
- **T-24h:** dispatch long gather trips to land after the window opens.
- **T-0:** claim trips inside the window, start scored actions, log the baseline.
- **T+24h:** compare score per resource, adjust, protect the reserve.
- **Final 6h:** close out, claim rewards, restart queues, rebuild the reserve.

<Callout type="strategy" title="Overlap beats volume">
A resource action that also completes a construction or research phase is worth more than twice as much. Check the live event tabs before spending.
</Callout>
