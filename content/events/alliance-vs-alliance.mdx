## What "Alliance vs Alliance" means here

Last Asylum: Plague uses several alliance-level competitive modes. This page focuses on the recurring **Alliance Duel** structure: a six-day, themed scoring competition reported by community guides as running Monday–Saturday, with a new scoring theme each day.

The event is not a single battle. It is a **week-long resource conversion contest**. Alliances that save deliberately and spend in the right window beat alliances with larger accounts that spend reactively.

## The six scoring phases

| Phase | Reported scoring sources | Primary stockpile | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raven | Raven upgrades, items, stamina | Raven materials, stamina | Spending stamina too early |
| Construction | Build completions, speed-ups, building Might | Build speed-ups, pre-staged timers | Timers completing outside the phase |
| Tech | Research, speed-ups, related chests | Research speed-ups, chests | Research starting too late |
| Hero | Recruitment, shards, upgrades | Shards, recruitment items | Chasing a banner without a plan |
| Preparation | Troop training and upgrades | Training speed-ups, resources | Queue capacity limits |
| Raid | PvP defeats / losses, combat | Stamina, heal speed-ups, rally readiness | Uncoordinated rallies |

## The single most important rule

**Saved stockpiles are worth more than completed upgrades.**

An upgrade completed on Sunday cannot score on Tuesday. A research timer that finishes at 23:50 the night before Tech phase is wasted AvA potential. The preparation discipline is simple:

- Freeze discretionary completions from Sunday reset.
- Pre-stage long timers so they can be finished inside the phase.
- Spend only the stockpile assigned to the current phase.
- Reserve a closer budget for the final hour.

## Win conditions beyond raw score

Daily phase wins and total score are the visible objectives, but strong alliances also compete on:

- **Efficiency** — points per speed-up or resource unit.
- **Coverage** — enough members able to score in every phase.
- **Morale** — fair recognition for lower-power contributors.
- **Intelligence** — knowing which phases the opponent historically contests.

## Raid phase: the coordination test

Raid is the phase where individual preparation meets alliance execution. The winning behaviours are boring and repeatable:

1. Confirm rally leads and backup leads before the phase opens.
2. Share target priorities and travel-time limits.
3. Use a single call channel. Duplicate rallies waste stamina.
4. Keep heal speed-ups for the second wave, not the first.
5. Screenshot battle reports for the after-action review.

If your alliance cannot run coordinated rallies, do not pretend Raid is a fair fight. Set a realistic target, protect your rank, and invest the difference in phases where you can win efficiently.

## After-action review

Within 24 hours of the duel ending, capture:

- Final score and placement.
- Points per phase.
- Top and bottom contributors by available stockpile.
- Which phase the opponent won and why.
- One process change for next week.

Store it with the other battle reports in the [image library](/gallery/). A wiki is only useful if the next plan is better than the last one.
