## The only question that matters

**What does this spend unlock?**

An event banner can make any upgrade look urgent. A useful spend does one of three things:

1. Unlocks a new skill or star breakpoint.
2. Fills a specific role gap in your main squad.
3. Completes a planned Alliance Duel / Survival Battle scoring window without damaging the roster.

If the answer is "it increases power a bit", it is not a plan.

## Breakpoint planning

| Situation | Better spend | Worse spend |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main squad lacks a front line | Tank hero breakpoint | Another damage hero at low stars |
| Next star tier is close | Finish the shard route | Spread shards across the roster |
| AvA Hero phase tomorrow | Hold recruitment items | Open them today for a small personal gain |
| Event reward tier is far | Stop and bank | Spend premium currency to bridge |

## Recruitment item discipline

Recruitment items are a scoring resource, not a slot machine. Hold them until:

- The live event scores recruitment or shard use.
- You know which hero you are targeting.
- Leadership has confirmed the AvA Hero phase timing (if applicable).

Opening items outside a scoring window is one of the most common unforced errors in the game.
